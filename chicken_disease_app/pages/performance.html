Sample Chicken Disease Categories

Salmonella

Infection causing diarrhea, fever, and abdominal cramps. Can spread rapidly among flocks if untreated.

Coccidiosis

A parasitic disease affecting chicken intestines, leading to poor growth, weakness, and death if severe.

New Castle Disease

Highly contagious viral infection causing respiratory distress, nervous symptoms, and reduced egg production.
