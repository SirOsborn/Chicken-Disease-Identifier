Project Objective

To develop, train, and evaluate a machine learning model capable of classifying chicken health status (specific diseases or healthy) based on images of their feces. This project aims to create a practical tool with potential real-world impact in poultry farming by enabling earlier disease detection.

Chicken Disease Overview

Our machine learning model is trained to identify health issues in chickens based on feces images. Here are some key areas we focus on to maintain poultry health and early disease detection.

Disease Detection

Analyzing patterns and symptoms through image recognition to detect abnormalities in feces samples.

Early Diagnosis

Providing early warning signs to help farmers take action before diseases spread widely among poultry.

Health Monitoring

Tracking chicken health over time to maintain a healthy farm environment and improve productivity.
